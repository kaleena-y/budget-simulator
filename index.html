Things nobody teaches you

The financial realities of student life

—

Rent is rarely your biggest problem

The real danger comes from the daily shuffle of groceries, transit, subscriptions, and coffee. Those small, quiet costs are usually what empty your account, not the fixed bills you already expected.

—

Paychecks feels bigger than they are

A $1,400 deposit looks massive on a Friday morning. Once you strip away basic survival expenses like rent and food, you might only have $200 of actual fun money left over.

—

Savings habits start small or not at all

Waiting until you have a massive income to save means you will keep waiting forever. Putting away just $30 a month builds a safety net faster than you think, especially when you see it stack up on screen.

—

The first budget is always wrong

That is completely normal. The goal here is not to be a perfect math wizard right away. It is just about getting comfortable looking at your numbers and tweaking them as life changes.
