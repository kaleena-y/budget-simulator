About BudgetU

Built for students.
Designed for clarity.

BudgetU is an interactive tool meant for people who want to wrap their heads around living on their own. We traded complex software spreadsheets for simple visuals so you can focus on learning how cash flows.

Why We Built This

A lot of money tools seem like they were designed for corporate accountants. We wanted to build something for the rest of us, whether you are moving into your first apartment, trying to make sense of your part-time income, or just curious about where your cash actually goes.


This simulator gives you a safe space to test things out. You can move sliders around, load a realistic lifestyle preset, and watch how it impacts your bottom line.

Our Values

Transparency first

We show all of our work. There are no mysterious background algorithms or confusing formulas here, just plain math you can easily follow.

No stress, no judgment

Real budgets fluctuate all the time. This space is meant for you to mess up, test bad decisions, and press reset whenever you want.

Built for real student life

We built our categories around actual student expenses like shared rent and streaming subscriptions, not generic financial textbook examples.

Privacy by design

We do not collect, save, or sell anything you type in. The entire app runs locally right inside your own browser window.
